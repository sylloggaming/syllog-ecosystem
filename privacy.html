Privacy Policy
How Syllog collects, uses, and protects your personal information.
Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Syllog. Syllog Games operates the browser-based gaming platform available at sylloggames.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

By creating a Syllog account or using our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information — Username, email address, and password when you create a Universal Syllog ID.
  • Profile Information — Profile photo, player preferences, and any information you voluntarily provide.
  • Payment Information — Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card details on our servers.
  • Usage Data — Game progress, XP, achievements, SC credit balance, session data, and in-game activity.
  • Technical Data — IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your Universal Syllog ID and player profile
  • Deliver and maintain the Syllog gaming platform and all connected titles
  • Process payments for Syllog Credits and Pass memberships via Stripe
  • Track cross-title XP, achievements, and leaderboard rankings
  • Send account-related emails including confirmations and support responses
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and cheating
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with row-level security. We use Cloudflare WAF, DDoS protection, and SSL encryption to protect all data in transit.

5. Cookies

  • Essential cookies — Required for authentication.
  • Analytics cookies — Help us understand platform usage.
  • Advertising cookies — Used by ad partners for free-tier players.

6. Advertising

Free-tier players may see ads served by AdinPlay. Gold and Platinum Pass subscribers have ads removed. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • Supabase — Player authentication and database
  • Cloudflare — Hosting, CDN, security
  • AdinPlay — Advertisement delivery

8. Children's Privacy

Syllog is not directed at children under 13. Contact us at support@sylloggames.com if you believe a child has provided personal information.

9. Your Rights

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your account and associated data
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications

Contact us at support@sylloggames.com to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Syllog after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

11. Contact Us
