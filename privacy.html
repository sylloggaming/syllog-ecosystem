Privacy Policy
How Syllog collects, uses, and protects your personal information.
Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Syllog ("we," "our," or "us"). Syllog Games operates the browser-based gaming platform available at sylloggames.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

By creating a Syllog account or using our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information — Username, email address, and password when you create a Universal Syllog ID.
  • Profile Information — Profile photo, player preferences, and any information you voluntarily provide.
  • Payment Information — When you purchase Syllog Credits or a Pass membership, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card details on our servers.
  • Usage Data — Game progress, XP, achievements, SC credit balance, session data, and in-game activity.
  • Technical Data — IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies used to deliver and improve our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your Universal Syllog ID and player profile
  • Deliver and maintain the Syllog gaming platform and all connected titles
  • Process payments for Syllog Credits and Pass memberships via Stripe
  • Track and display cross-title XP, achievements, and leaderboard rankings
  • Send account-related emails including confirmations and support responses
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and cheating
  • Improve our games and platform based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a PostgreSQL-based platform with row-level security. All SC balances, item drops, and progression updates are computed server-side. We use Cloudflare's Web Application Firewall (WAF), DDoS protection, and SSL encryption to protect all data in transit.

5. Cookies

  • Essential cookies — Required for the platform to function, including authentication tokens.
  • Analytics cookies — Help us understand how players interact with our games and platform.
  • Advertising cookies — Used by our advertising partners to serve relevant ads to free-tier players.

6. Advertising

Free-tier players may see advertisements served by third-party networks including AdinPlay. Gold and Platinum Pass subscribers have ads removed from their entitled games. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • Supabase — Player authentication and database storage
  • Cloudflare — Website hosting, CDN, security, and domain management
  • AdinPlay — Advertisement delivery for free-tier players

8. Children's Privacy

Syllog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at [email protected].

9. Your Rights

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your account and associated data
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [email protected].

10. Changes to This Policy
