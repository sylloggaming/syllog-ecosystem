Privacy Policy
How Syllog collects, uses, and protects your personal information.
Last updated: April 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Syllog ("we," "our," or "us"). Syllog Games. operates the browser-based gaming platform available at sylloggames.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

By creating a Syllog account or using our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information — Username, email address, and password when you create a Universal Syllog ID.
  • Profile Information — Profile photo, player preferences, and any information you voluntarily provide.
  • Payment Information — When you purchase Syllog Credits or a Pass membership, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card details on our servers.
  • Usage Data — Game progress, XP, achievements, SC credit balance, session data, and in-game activity.
  • Technical Data — IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies used to deliver and improve our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Universal Syllog ID and player profile
  • Deliver and maintain the Syllog gaming platform and all connected titles
  • Process payments for Syllog Credits and Pass memberships via Stripe
  • Track and display cross-title XP, achievements, and leaderboard rankings
  • Send account-related emails including confirmations and support responses
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and cheating
  • Improve our games and platform based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a PostgreSQL-based platform with row-level security. This means each player can only access their own data — no API call can access another player's account, SC balance, or inventory.

All SC balances, item drops, and progression updates are computed server-side. The browser client only renders what our server authorizes. We use Cloudflare's Web Application Firewall (WAF), DDoS protection, and SSL encryption to protect all data in transit.

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and analyze platform usage. These include:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the platform to function, including authentication tokens.
  • Analytics cookies — Help us understand how players interact with our games and platform.
  • Advertising cookies — Used by our advertising partners (including Google AdSense) to serve relevant ads to free-tier players.

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect platform functionality.

6. Advertising

Free-tier players may see advertisements served by third-party networks including Google AdSense and AdinPlay. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your interests. Gold and Platinum Pass subscribers have ads removed from their entitled games.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers. Ad networks operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Syllog:

  • Stripe — Payment processing for SC Credits and Pass subscriptions
  • Supabase — Player authentication and database storage
  • Cloudflare — Website hosting, CDN, security, and domain management
  • Google AdSense — Advertisement delivery for free-tier players

Each of these services has their own privacy policy governing their use of your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Syllog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@sylloggames.com and we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your account and associated data
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@sylloggames.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of Syllog after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: support@sylloggames.com
  • Website: sylloggames.com
