Privacy Policy
How Syllog collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Syllog Games operates the browser-based gaming platform at sylloggames.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information — Username, email, and password when you create a Universal Syllog ID.
  • Payment Information — Processed by Stripe. We never store full card details on our servers.
  • Usage Data — Game progress, XP, SC balance, session data, and in-game activity.
  • Technical Data — IP address, browser type, device info, and cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your Universal Syllog ID and player profile
  • Deliver and maintain the Syllog gaming platform and all titles
  • Process payments for Syllog Credits and Pass memberships
  • Track cross-title XP, achievements, and leaderboard rankings
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud and cheating

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Supabase with row-level security. We use Cloudflare WAF and SSL encryption to protect all data in transit.

5. Advertising

Free-tier players may see ads served by AdinPlay. Gold and Platinum Pass subscribers have ads removed. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

6. Children's Privacy

Syllog is not directed at children under 13. Contact [email protected] if you believe a child has provided personal information.

7. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
