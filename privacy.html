Privacy Policy
How Syllog collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Syllog Games operates the browser-based gaming platform at sylloggames.com. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information — Username, email, and password for your Universal Syllog ID.
  • Payment Information — Processed by Stripe. We never store full card details.
  • Usage Data — Game progress, XP, SC balance, and in-game activity.
  • Technical Data — IP address, browser type, and cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your Universal Syllog ID and player profile
  • Deliver and maintain the Syllog gaming platform
  • Process payments for Syllog Credits and Pass memberships
  • Track XP, achievements, and leaderboard rankings
  • Detect and prevent fraud and cheating

4. Data Security

Your data is stored using Supabase with row-level security. We use Cloudflare WAF and SSL encryption to protect all data.

5. Advertising

Free-tier players may see ads via AdinPlay. Gold and Platinum subscribers have ads removed. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
