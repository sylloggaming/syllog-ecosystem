■ Void Rift — Origin

How humanity's greatest achievement became its greatest mistake.
■ 2841 CE — Deep Space Research Vessel SYL-7 Prometheus
Someone opened the wrong gate.

For 200 years, humanity used the Void Gates — ancient technology discovered buried beneath a dying star — to travel between star systems in seconds. No one built them. No one knew how old they were. They simply existed, waiting.

The Galactic Annihilation Force (GAF) was formed to protect the gate network. Ten thousand worlds connected. Civilization expanded. Everything was fine.

Until the Prometheus crew detected something impossible in the Kaelthar Belt — a gate that wasn't on any map, pulsing with deep violet energy unlike anything ever recorded.

Against orders, they activated it.

What came through was not a ship. Not a fleet. It was a sound — a frequency that traveled through the gate, through space, through the hull, through the bodies of every crew member — and rewrote them at a molecular level.

The Prometheus went dark 11 seconds later.

■ GAF INCIDENT REPORT #7741 — CLASSIFIED ■
Three days after the incident, the Prometheus drifted back through the gate — empty. Every surface inside was covered in a crystalline black substance. At the center of the bridge, carved into the floor with surgical precision, was a single symbol.

The Void Rune. Scientists still don't know what it means.

Commander Kael Voss

Kael was not supposed to survive. When Void forces overran the Kaelthar Belt forward base, every other pilot launched to intercept. They were all destroyed or captured within minutes. Kael's ship had a catastrophic systems failure during launch — a mundane malfunction that kept him grounded while everyone else died.

He watched it all from the hangar. Alone. By the time his systems came back online, there was no one left to call. No command structure. No orders. No allies.

He launched anyway.

"What drives Kael is not heroism. Not duty. Something simpler and more honest — he has nothing else. The fight is not about winning anymore. It's about making the Sovereign pay for every life it took, one enemy at a time, for as long as he can stay alive."
■ Void Rift — Enemy Codex
The Void Armies
The Sovereign does not fight with ships or soldiers in any conventional sense. It corrupts. Every enemy was once something else.
⬢
■ The Special
Void Golems
Purple Hex Guardians
Once the automated defense drones of humanity's earliest gate stations. Corrupted by Void Energy, their programming replaced with a single directive: destroy the desecrators. Their crystalline shells are nearly impenetrable.
■ First appeared at Gate Station Kepler-7, three days after the Prometheus incident. Every drone simultaneously stopped, turned to face the gate, and walked into it. They came back wrong.
👑
■ The Elite
Crystal Predators
Teal Hunters
Once the GAF's most advanced fighter pilots — ace soldiers captured during early engagements. The Bleaching process was only partial, preserving their tactical intelligence while overwriting their loyalty. They retained their skills. They lost everything else.
■ GAF Command issued Order 77 — any pilot captured by Void forces is to be considered dead. No rescue missions. No negotiations.
💥
■ The Grunt
Void Drones
Mass-Produced Shock Troops
Raw asteroid rock and salvaged ship debris, bleached and animated by Void Energy. No intelligence. No purpose beyond swarming. The Sovereign produces them by the millions — they are the tide that overwhelms by sheer numbers.
■ After the fall of Arcturus Colony, a debris field 40,000 km wide began moving against orbital mechanics. 3 million strong — walking through space.
⚡
■ The Zig
Phase Reavers
Erratic Dimension-Hoppers
Once quantum research AIs designed to probe the void dimension. The Sovereign repurposed them as psychological weapons. They phase partially into the void dimension, making them appear to teleport unpredictably.
■ GAF pilots who survived extended Zig combat reported nightmares for months. The patterns burned into their visual cortex. Some couldn't fly straight lines anymore.

■ Primary Antagonist
The Void Sovereign
An entity of incomprehensible age and power that exists simultaneously in normal space and in the dimension between dimensions. It is a consciousness woven into the fabric of void energy itself — ancient beyond measurement, patient beyond human comprehension. It has watched galaxies form and collapse.

The gates were never technology. They are wounds — tears in the membrane between dimensions caused by a catastrophe so ancient that no living being remembers it. The Sovereign has spent millions of years as their guardian.

Humanity ripped them open. Used them as subway stations. The Sovereign's response is not rage. It is correction.
■ Classified — Dr. Mira Solen Files
The Four Skins — What They Really Are
Aurora Skin — Prismatic Deflection Array. Crystallized light-refraction particles that make the ship's outline unpredictable to Void targeting. "The one beautiful thing I made during the war." — Dr. Solen

Inferno Skin — Thermal Aggression Plating. Superheated ablative armor burning at 4,000 degrees. Void Energy has difficulty binding to extreme heat sources.

Nebula Skin — Void-Resonance Dampener. Hull frequency that mimics the energy signature of dead matter — cosmic debris. The ship becomes harder to detect.

Celestial Wraith — Void-Phase Integration. Void Energy integrated directly into the hull matrix. The ship exists partially in the void dimension at all times. The Sovereign can sense it. But nothing hits harder.
■ Story Truth — Classified
What Kael Doesn't Know
Kael's ship malfunction wasn't random. The Sovereign caused it. The Sovereign let him live on purpose.

Because it needs something from him. The catastrophe that created the gates — the wound the Sovereign has guarded for millions of years — it can only be healed by a human.

The Sovereign didn't spare Kael to be merciful. It spared him because he's the key.
■ Three Endings — Classified
How Kael's Story Ends
Ending 1 — The Sacrifice (True Ending)
Kael's human life force — willingly given — seals the original wound. The Void armies dissolve. The gates go dark. Humanity is safe. Kael is gone. Somewhere in Zone 8, Dr. Solen receives one last transmission — just static, and beneath it, something that might be his voice saying "it's done."

Ending 2 — The Defiance
Kael destroys the Sovereign's physical manifestation and escapes. The wound remains open. The Void will return. But humanity has time. Maybe enough.

Ending 3 — The Corruption (Secret)
Fly the Celestial Wraith into the final battle. The Void doesn't fight him — it offers. Kael can become what the Sovereign is. Guardian of the wound. Immortal. Alone. The Void armies stop. All of them. Simultaneously.
