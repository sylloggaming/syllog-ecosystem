Quick Answers
How do I reset my password?
On the Sign In page, click "Forgot password" and enter your email. A reset link will be sent to you.
Can I get a refund on SC Credits?
All purchases are generally final. Contact us within 48 hours of purchase for exceptional circumstances.
How do I cancel my Pass?
Email support@sylloggames.com with your account details and we'll process the cancellation immediately.
My progress isn't saving — what do I do?
Make sure you're signed in to your Syllog ID. Progress only saves for logged-in players.
