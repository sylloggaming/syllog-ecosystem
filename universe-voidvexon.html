■ Mission Brief
The Last Signal
For three centuries the Void consumed galaxies. The Galactic Annihilation Force burned. One transmission remains — coordinates to the wound where Darkness was born.
YEAR 2847 — OPENING CRAWL

The universe is dying.

An ancient force known only as THE VOID has consumed galaxy after galaxy — silent, absolute, without mercy. Stars extinguished. Civilisations erased. Realities folded into darkness and forgotten.

The great fleets of the Galactic Annihilation Force were assembled to hold the line. Two thousand warships. The finest pilots across twelve star systems. They fought. They burned. They fell.

Seventeen hours ago, a dying AI transmission named the place: VOID VEXON — the wound in reality where the darkness was born. One hundred fifty waves of void creatures. Thirty warlords. And at the end, THE ETERNAL VOID — Darkness older than stars.

The dead are watching. The stars are watching. Everything that ever lived is counting on the last pilot.

Before the beginning. After the end. It does not wage war for territory — territory is a surface idea. It unmakes. When Commander Vael Rhynn finally breaks it, stars rekindle… and its dying scream travels outward as a signal to whatever it was guarding against.

The Herald Doctrine
Why the Eternal Void Fought
GAF believed the Void was conquest. Classified cosmography says otherwise: The Eternal Void was a chained sentinel, burning galaxies as fuel to keep a older door sealed. Every warlord was a symptom. Darkness was the lock.

Why Rhynn Survived
The malfunction that spared the Commander was not luck. Something on both sides of the wound needed a human will at the threshold — a key that could choose. Season One ends when that key turns. Season Two begins when the door answers.
