Questions
Frequently Asked
Are all games really free to play?▼
Yes — every single Syllog title is free to play forever, with no exceptions. Passes add bonus value on top of the free experience. Revenue comes from player choice, never compulsion.
What are Syllog Credits (SC)?▼
Syllog Credits are a single premium currency purchased once and valid across all Syllog games. Your balance is never locked to one game — it travels with your Universal Syllog ID everywhere.
How does the annual plan work?▼
You pay once upfront for the full year and get 2 months free compared to monthly billing. Gold Annual is $79.99 (vs $95.88 monthly). Platinum Annual is $149.99 (vs $179.88 monthly). Your SC gift is distributed monthly throughout the year.
Can I cancel my Pass at any time?▼
Yes. Monthly passes can be cancelled any time and remain active until the end of your billing period. Annual passes can be cancelled and remain active for the remainder of the year you paid for. No penalties, no questions.
Do SC Credits expire?▼
Free player credits expire after 12 months of inactivity. Gold and Platinum subscribers’ credits never expire while the subscription is active.
What happens to my Pass if I miss a payment?▼
If a payment fails, Stripe will automatically retry. If unsuccessful, your Pass will downgrade to Free Player. Your SC balance, XP, and progress are never deleted — you keep everything you earned.
Is Gold Pass really worth it?▼
At $7.99/month you receive 1,000 SC which has a retail value of ~$8.30 — the pass pays for itself in credits alone before ad removal, early access, or the purchase bonus are counted.
Can I upgrade from Gold to Platinum later?▼
Yes — you can upgrade at any time. The price difference will be prorated and you will be charged only for the remaining time in your billing period. Your benefits upgrade immediately.
